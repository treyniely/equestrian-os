A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
get in the saddle.

Who is EquestrianOS built for?

Lesson businesses and riding academies that need to coordinate riders, trainers, horses and riding spaces, alongside payments and customer communications.

How will the 14-day trial work?

The planned trial gives you 14 days to explore the software and set up your academy with guided onboarding. You’ll choose a subscription plan to continue afterward. Final plans and trial terms will be confirmed before launch.

Can riders book their own lessons?

The planned rider portal will let riders and families view availability and book eligible lessons, subject to your academy’s availability, suitability and booking policies.

Can instructors share educational content?

The planned learning hub supports instructor-created videos, guides, assignments and short knowledge checks. Riders and families can follow their progress, while practical skills require instructor sign-off. Paid course sales are outside the initial scope.

Will there be mobile access?

Mobile experiences for both business teams and riders are part of the product roadmap. This first preview shows the intended experience; native apps are not available yet.

Can I watch a demo without booking a call?

Yes—that’s the plan. An on-demand recorded demo will be available before launch. For now, the Watch demo button opens an interactive walkthrough of the product concept.
