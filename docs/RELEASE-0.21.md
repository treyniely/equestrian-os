# Release 0.21 — Offerings, plans and bookable allowances

Lessons & Services separates Step 1 (what customers book) from Step 2 (plans and packages). Custom is the eighth offering template. Every offering can be named, edited and priced with its own duration, capacity, mounting and suitability settings. Choose a drop-in price (also includable in plans), plan allowance required, or free for everyone. Private parties retain their organizer flow and are excluded from allowance bundles.

Plans bundle quantities of actual offerings: e.g. $190/month for four riding lessons and four horsemanship classes. Each named allowance may cover several interchangeable offerings sharing one quantity. A single offering cannot appear in multiple allowances in the same plan. Plans can apply to one rider or be shared by a family. Monthly plans have billing-date periods; one-time packs have a configurable validity duration. Free-for-everyone offerings are excluded from paid bundles to prevent misleading limits. Currency is USD; prices use integer minor units.

Customers → Manage → Plans & included sessions starts a plan or pack for the correct family/rider. Staff confirm an externally paid or complimentary period before activation. Monthly renewals explicitly record the next paid period. This grants booking rights; it does not charge a card, create a Stripe subscription or verify receipt of external funds. Automatic renewals, webhooks, refunds, purchase checkout, rollover, proration and unlimited plans are excluded. Legacy name-only memberships remain readable/manageable but do not grant unlimited booking access. No legacy records are automatically converted to paid entitlements.

The family portal displays allowance balances on Home and Account. Bookings use the period containing the lesson's start time, not the date the parent clicked Book. Eligible sessions show their allowance; exhausted/member-only sessions cannot be booked. Drop-in payment without credit still requires staff help because checkout is unconnected. Admin enrollment into member-only offerings also requires an allowance. Admin-paid drop-in registrations retain their manual-record path. Each confirmed registration spends one allowance, regardless of activity duration; businesses define separate allowances for different durations if appropriate. Timely customer cancellation or staff registration removal returns that credit. Academy cancellation releases usage while preserving the registration history. Past/no-show attendance does not automatically restore a credit.

Rescheduling releases the original credit and consumes a target credit inside one transaction; failure preserves the original registration. A matching target period must exist. Exact-limit rescheduling can reuse the original credit. Database insertion triggers recheck the current membership status, family/rider scope, offering, lesson-date period and remaining quantity. A portal-only funding requirement prevents concurrent paid-offering requests from falling back to unpaid drop-in booking when the final credit is consumed. Capacity/resource/rider guards and rollback remain shared. Month-end renewals retain the original billing-day anchor across short months. Purchased period prices/allowances are immutable snapshots; changing a plan affects future activations only. Deleted offerings prevent new activation until inclusions are repaired; existing snapshots/history remain.

Architecture: domain/plans defines validation, local-calendar period arithmetic and UI eligibility; server/plans owns staff-confirmed grants and balance reads. No new dependencies or provider SDKs. Plan definitions reuse tenant-scoped resources with kind=plan. Generated migrations 0014_freezing_loki and 0015_magical_colossus add normalized benefit periods, allowance buckets, offering mappings, credit usage and transactional funding requirements. Schema-only SQL includes indexed usage lookup, a balance view, overlap and enrollment-credit triggers. All mutation routes enforce billing management, and responses hide plans/balances from staff without billing visibility. Family responses contain only that family's allowances and registrations. The narrow transient funding table is cleared in the same booking transaction; it is not a payment ledger.
