# Release 0.19 — Plan lessons from available time

The default monthly planner now starts with the selected trainer's saved working hours. Select a riding space, click an opening and choose an existing offering. Its configured duration consumes that time: a 30-minute lesson at 9:00 leaves an opening at 9:30; a 90-minute group lesson then leaves 11:00. New exact placements never move another session. Trainer/business hours, time off, lunch, existing reservations and proposed sessions constrain the available intervals. Custom lesson/activity names, durations and capacity come from Lessons & Services.

Each day separates open time, proposed sessions and already-live work. Click a proposed session to change its start/offering or remove it. Fill open time repeats a chosen offering only where complete sessions fit, preserving breaks and bookings. Copy day or copy week lets staff select actual target dates/weeks and inspect skipped conflicts before confirming. Copying duplicates proposed slots only, never live sessions, riders or registrations. Optional supporting interns are checked for availability and recorded absence. One-step Undo applies to additions, edits, fills, removals and copies. Review & publish remains the existing server-reviewed atomic workflow; nothing is bookable until published.

Preservation: the complete 0.18 weekly/date-hours planner remains under Classic weekly planner. Both views share the same in-memory draft. Classic blocks are edited in Classic; individual exact placements are edited in From availability. No old component, deployed version or published lesson is deleted.
